The problems we took off people's desks.

Five operations. Each one had a job that belonged to a person and should have belonged to a system.

01 Auto-parts wholesale 02 Food and beverage distribution 03 Construction materials 04 Aesthetic and beauty care 05 Real estate agency
01

Auto-parts wholesale

The root problem

Every request for a part ran through a person's hands five times. Identify it in the manufacturer catalog, check it in the aftermarket catalog, find it in the ERP, compare suppliers in eight separate portals, type the order. The counter was a lookup desk. The customer waited while it happened.

What we put in

Agents on top of the ERP they already ran. Part identification, inventory, and procurement: it watches stock, compares the suppliers they already buy from on price and availability, prepares the purchase, and raises it when stock falls. Customer replies, sales, finance alongside. No new system, no new tool for staff.

What's different

The lookup is done by the time the customer finishes typing. The person at the counter decides which supplier and which price. That is the whole job now.

02

Food and beverage distribution

The root problem

Four jobs that all run through the same few people. Orders arrive by phone and message and get retyped. Stock is counted after it has already run out. Procurement is a person remembering what to reorder. Operations is whoever is free, chasing whichever of the three broke that morning.

What we put in

Agents across order intake, stock, procurement and the day-to-day operations that connect them, sitting on top of the systems the distributor already runs. An order that arrives is read, checked against stock, confirmed. Stock below threshold triggers the purchase. The handoffs between them stop needing a person.

What's different

The team stops being the glue between four systems. They handle the exceptions and the customers. The routine runs.

03

Construction materials

The root problem

A catalog in the millions of items and a person expected to know it. Finding the right product meant searching by memory, by supplier catalog, by a phone call to the supplier. Stock across that many lines was never current. Procurement ran on what somebody noticed was missing.

What we put in

Agents on top of the systems they already run. A product finder that identifies the right item from a description, a code or a photo, across the whole catalog. Inventory that keeps the count current. Procurement that compares suppliers and raises the purchase when stock falls.

What's different

“Do we have it, and if not, where do we get it” is answered while the customer is still on the line. The team sells. The catalog stopped living in someone's head.

04

Aesthetic and beauty care

The root problem

The phone. It rings while both hands are in a treatment, during lunch, after closing. Some of those calls were bookings. Nobody knew how many, because missed calls do not leave a record.

What we put in

The Receptionist. It answers every call in Albanian or English, whichever the caller uses, knows the services and the real calendar, books, moves or cancels the appointment, confirms it, and sends the reminder.

What's different

Every call gets answered. Nobody's hands leave the client.

05

Real estate agency

The root problem

Three jobs that ate the day between viewings. Every inquiry on a listing answered by hand, from the website, the portals, social. Every new listing posted and reposted across the channels, one by one. Every deal's documents assembled, sent, chased, filed. The agents sold property in the gaps.

What we put in

Agents on top of the tools they already used: their website, their social accounts, their calendar, Gmail and Drive. One answers listing inquiries and books viewings into the agents' calendars. One takes a new listing and publishes it across the website and social channels. One prepares each deal's documents, sends them, tracks what comes back, and files it.

What's different

The inquiry is answered while the agent is at a viewing. New listings are everywhere at once. The paperwork is done before anyone sits down to do it. The agents are back to selling property.
