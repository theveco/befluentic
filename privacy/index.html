Privacy

How we handle your data.

Interim notice

A full privacy notice prepared by our counsel is on its way and will replace this page. Until then, this is our working summary. It already reflects how we actually handle your data.

What we collect

Your name, company, email, and whatever you tell us through the contact form or the AI Readiness Check. Nothing more.

Why

To reply, judge whether we are a fit, and prepare for a call. We do not use it for anything else.

What we never do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. No marketing lists. No sharing with third parties for advertising.

Services we use

A small set of services processes data on our behalf to run this site and answer you:

  • Formspree · delivers your form submissions to our inbox
  • Calendly · schedules calls you book with us
  • Vercel · hosts this website
  • Cloudflare · network delivery and security
  • Airtable Inc. · manages prospect and client records

Analytics

We measure page traffic with a privacy-first tool that sets no cookies and builds no personal profiles.

Retention and control

We keep enquiries while we are in contact and remove them on request. Email info@befluentic.com to see, correct, or delete what we hold.

Governing law

For data subjects in Switzerland, this notice is additionally governed by the Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) of 25 September 2020, as revised.
